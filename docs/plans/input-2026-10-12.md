# The pointer, the keys and the ring, made one

*Decided with the owner on 2026-10-12, one question at a time. Today the
view's orbit controls pan on a right-drag, and the ring opens on the
browser's context-menu event, which a right-drag's release also raises: a
pan summons the ring. Fractal zoom -- stepping into a part's level and back
out -- happens on a double-click and automatically when the wheel pushes
past the dolly limit, with no key and nothing to adjust.*

## Decided

- **An unmodified right-click is the camera's.** A right-drag pans. A
  still right-click re-centres the view on the surface point under the
  pointer: the orbit's pivot moves there, so the next turn orbits around
  it; on empty space it does nothing. Neither summons the ring.
- **Ctrl + right-click summons the ring**, the modifier configurable. On
  macOS, Ctrl+click is already a right-click, so Ctrl+click works there.
- **The ring keeps a key and its button.** The key is a configurable
  shortcut, `m` by default, opening the ring on the selection; the header's
  ⌗ Ring button stays as the visible way in.
- **Fractal zoom has shortcuts**: Page Down steps into the selected part,
  or the one nearest the view's centre; Page Up steps out to the level
  above. Both configurable. Double-click stays a way in; Backspace stays a
  way out.
- **Automatic zoom has settings**: on or off; stepping in and stepping out
  each on or off on its own; how much extra scroll past the limit it takes
  before a step; and where the limit sits -- how close to a part, and how
  far out from a level, the camera may dolly before the limit is reached.
- **One Settings panel, kept per browser.** Every shortcut and navigation
  setting lives in a Settings panel (a rail tab, opened from ⚙ View ›
  Settings and its ring cell), each with a reset to its default, kept in
  this browser's storage.

## The overview

The same round looks at every way the page takes input -- the mouse
buttons and wheel, the keys, the ring's cells, the gestures on handles and
badges -- for conflicts, for one action reached two different ways that
disagree, for a binding nothing on screen tells a person about, and for the
words the hint bar and the docs use. What it finds that a decision above
does not settle comes to the owner before it is built.

## Decided after the overview

The overview mapped every binding on the page and found where they did not
hold together. Asked of the owner on 2026-10-13, one question at a time,
with the conventional answer taken where one was plain:

- **One right-button rule everywhere.** Ctrl + right-click (the configured
  modifier) is the ring in the world, on a Contents row, on a badge and in a
  Machine; a plain right-click is the camera's in the world and the
  browser's everywhere else. Built on pointer events -- a still press and
  release -- not on the context-menu event, which some systems raise on the
  press; the modifier is heard before the view's controls turn it into a
  rotate.
- **A click is a still left press and release**; the middle and right
  buttons never select.
- **Clearing the selection**: a still click on empty canvas clears it, and
  so does Escape, the last rung of one Escape ladder (close a menu or the
  ring, cancel a drag in progress with nothing saved, then deselect).
- **Shift+M opens the canvas ring** whatever is selected; `m` opens the
  selection's. Both configurable.
- **The page names its essentials only**: the hint bar stays one line, and
  the Settings panel is the reference for every binding. No separate card.
- **Ring addresses hold still**: cells that come and go are seated after
  the constant ones, so each constant cell keeps one address everywhere.
- **Confirming by consequence**: undoable in the page, no question; not
  undoable, press twice, the control saying "again to …"; heats, moves or
  flashes a machine, a dialog; the emergency stop, no question ever.
- **The words**: changing level is **Step in** and **Step out** everywhere;
  the node ring's Up is **Parent** and its Into is **Inside**; a thing in
  the world is a **piece** in every visible string; the site's Reset is
  **Rebuild** and a board's is **Reboot**; ⚙ View uses the ring's labels.
  "Zoom" is the wheel's.
- **Keys act where they belong**: one check decides whether a shortcut
  fires (never while typing); Page Up and Page Down step levels only when
  the world has the keys and scroll everywhere else; the ring key and the
  rail's `~` are matched by key position as well, for other layouts.
- **While a part's editor is open, Ctrl+S anywhere saves the variant**;
  F5 and F6 stay inside the editor.
- **`f` frames the selection, else the level; Shift+F always the level.**
- **A still long press is the ring on touch.**
- **The part editor's bar is pinned only while editing**; otherwise Selected
  shows the piece's name, position and facts first.
- Taken as the overview proposed, needing no question: the world takes the
  keyboard focus (a focus ring; arrows pan, Shift+arrows turn, only while it
  has it); the status line is announced to screen readers and focus comes
  back after Selected redraws; one step-in rule for the wheel, Page Down,
  double-click and the ring (the selection, else what the view's centre
  ray hits, walls passed through), saying what it did; and automatic zoom
  ignores a trackpad's momentum.
