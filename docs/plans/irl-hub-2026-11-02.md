# An irl hub of ShowRunner's class: a plan

*This page orders the hub's work, names what each member has and what it needs,
and ends with the owner's questions. The owner has decided that apothecary is the
hub; what is built of it is [docs/family.md](../family.md).*

## What this plans

The show-control family has a hub, ShowRunner. It holds a show: a script with
cues on its lines, each cue on a layer with its notes and its actions. When a
cue fires, its family plugin hands each member its part of the cue's `family`
block. The contract that makes that work across the estate's families is
hearth ([quaternionmedia/hearth](https://github.com/quaternionmedia/hearth)):
`family/1`, four routes on a member and three on a hub, over HTTP, JSON and
server-sent events; a member library; the coordinator as one text an exemplar
imports or copies whole; and `hearth check`, which judges a member or a hub
by its routes alone. Its demo is Loopback, nine cues ShowRunner runs alone,
with ShowStopper, carlos, joe and leo beside it. hearth is a week on its
`main`, carries a development version and no tag, and its adoption record on
the corpus's `project/hearth` branch names a second exemplar as the thing
that tests its scope.

The irl family has no hub. Its members drive matter: parts, boards, enclosures
and the firmware that makes one work (the family record,
`governance/qm/records/DRAFT-a-family-is-bordered-by-what-it-drives.md`). What
irl needs is not a script and a queue of cues but a blueprint and the steps
that bring it into working order: print this, fit it, flash that, plug a lead,
close a contact, hold the rig in carlos, read the faces, run the checks.
Systems integration, in a word.

This plans an irl hub of ShowRunner's class: apothecary as hearth's second
exemplar, running the coordinator imported from `qm-hearth`, with a procedure
of steps on a blueprint in place of a cue list on a script. Each step's family
block is delivered to the family's members over `family/1`, and every outcome
is kept in the evidence log beside the polls, frames and readings it changed.
The members include the mic and the camera's video: joe, with vox inside it,
and alfred, with otto inside it, reached across the seam as carlos is.

## The members

other by URL: hearth's hosting rule, and the family record's §5, two families
meet across a protocol. The table is the hub's `[family]` table, kept in the
state folder beside the pins, and `docs/configuration.md` names where. Every
member reached by URL is on this machine: the guard in `apothecary/stays_local.py`
refuses any other address, as it does for carlos today.

scad-chess, whose deliverable is a printed set and which runs nothing; leo,

**The bus.** datum speaks MQTT, and frizzle is the organisation's dispatcher
on it: a journal of `qm/#`, work-queue semantics, a collector, with its
durable queue and rule engine not yet built. The hub publishes nothing to it
and reads nothing from it in this plan; datum's stand-in subscribes to datum's
topics alone, and the evidence log is the journal. When frizzle's queue holds
across a restart, the hub's outcomes can be journalled there as well.

## What fires

ShowRunner fires on GO, and the stage asserts. The irl hub fires on GO too,
and adds the one thing a show has no use for: evidence arriving on its own. A
print finishes, a board answers on a port, a contact closes, a frame shows a
plug. A step names the evidence it waits for as a subject and a property the
log already carries (`garage:printer_1#state` is `idle`; `usb:A106ZTEU` is
`present`; `garage:datum_1#contact` is `closed`), and the collector that
writes that observation tells the procedure. The step then stands by, and the
Procedure window says so.

Whether a standing-by step fires itself is the step's, set by a person, and
bounded:

- A step that moves matter -- heats, prints, jogs, homes, flashes a board --
  is GO-only. Its member keeps apothecary's own bounds under it: the control
  latch a person arms, `JOG_MAX_MM`, the firmware's soft endstops after a
  homing.
- A step that changes a record may follow: a hold sent to carlos, a frame
  kept, a reading, a check, a cut, a word said.

A fire is hearth's: the step's cue (`number`, `name`, `fired_at`) and its
family block; one queue a member, so blocks arrive in the order they fired;
every outcome kept; a member down, slow or refusing recorded and never
raised; and a refused step changing nothing.

## What is kept

Each delivery's outcome, as the coordinator records it -- the member, how it
was reached, the status, the milliseconds from the fire, the instance that
took it, the error -- goes into the evidence log as a fourth kind beside
observations, polls and frames: `actions/<day>.jsonl`, each naming its step
and the subject the step sat on. The Working space's *what changed* column,
which the working-space plan gives the actions and directives nearest each
transition, reads them from there. The hub's `GET /family/` is the same
record for the recent ones, live.

## The window

A **Procedure** window, registered as every window is, under Panels › Site ›
Procedure, within the ring's twelve characters. It lists the procedure's steps
in order, each with its discipline, the node it sits on, whether it is
waiting, standing by, fired, done, refused or failed, and the outcome of its
last fire per member; GO on the step standing by; and the member panel each
outcome opens: the printer's Machine, the board's badge, carlos's rack in a
tab. Nothing else moves: the world, the Working space and the Machine stay
what they are. A procedure is kept in the state folder, one file a procedure
under its site.

## The check

`hearth check --hub` against `apothecary serve`, and `hearth check
<hub>/<member>` against each hosted member, each sent one real action against
the simulated printer and devkit. Apothecary pins the conformance version it
replayed, and the corpus's `ci/interop-registry.yaml` gains the `family/1`
surface with hearth as publisher and apothecary as an implementation of the
hub side, so `uv run qm interop` compares them (hearth's adoption record, §3).

The corpus's gate across a family is `uv run qm family-demo`. An `irl` phase
joins it with apothecary running and the rest declared with what each needs,
the denominator the whole family, a stub reported as not implemented and
never as a pass. Two things apothecary needs for that contract: `serve --state
DIR`, which `apothecary serve` lacks (its state folder is the environment's),
and a `check --url` that is not `apothecary check`, which lists what is
installed. The entry `apothecary family` carries both: `family serve` and
`family check`.

The walkthrough page is hermetic, as hearth's own coordinator page is: the
members in the process, the simulated printer and devkit, the coordinator
fired by GO and the outcomes read back. The e2e tests with a real carlos
(`CARLOS_CHECKOUT`) hold the seam, and the datum build is the first procedure
to run end to end (*The demo*).

## Order

Each is one pull request, stacked where it depends on the one before.

1. **The hub stands.** `qm-hearth` as a dependency, a git source at a pinned
   revision until hearth's first tag; `apothecary/family/`: the coordinator
   from its table, `coordinator.router` on `/family/*`,
   `identity("apothecary", "irl", version)`; carlos a push member in the
   table, `wiring/carlos.py`'s Follower firing through the coordinator so the
   hold is audited; `hearth check --hub` green; the conformance version pinned
   and the registry row beside it, since the row claims that pin.
2. **Steps on the blueprint.** The procedure and step models, their file under
   the site, the disciplines, the standby condition as a subject and a
   property, routes to list a procedure and GO a step, the outcomes into
   `actions/`; the Procedure window.
3. **The hub's own members.** A machine and a board are built, hosted under
   `/<name>/family/*` with `hearth.web.router`, the long verbs answering 202 and
   `hearth check` green on each. The reader is next, and a camera is read from the
   page.
4. **The mic and the camera's video.** joe's `listen` and `say`, alfred's
   `cut`, each in its own repository, with apothecary's side a row in the
   table and a step; until each lands, its row answers not implemented.
5. **The switch and the light.** datum's stand-in over MQTT and ira's `show`;
   the datum build as a procedure: print `datum_core`, fit it, flash the YAML,
   close the contact, see the event, ira showing it.
6. **The show and the bench meet.** ShowRunner's `go`, and apothecary in
   ShowRunner's table, so the concert plan's readings tie to the show's cues
   (its slice 5).
7. **The gate.** The `irl` phase in `ci/family-demo.yaml`.

## Decided

The owner's, on 2026-11-02:

- The irl family gets a hub of ShowRunner's class, built on hearth's contract
  and its coordinator.
- In place of scripts and queues of cues, the hub works from blueprints and
  integrates systems: a step sits on a node of the site as drawn, and what it
  fires brings that node into working order.
- joe with vox, and alfred with otto, are members of the hub, for its hooks
  into the mic and the camera: a person's word spoken at the bench, speech
  back, and a video cut from the frames the cameras keep.

And on 2026-11-05:

- Apothecary is the irl family's hub, its hearth: hearth's second exemplar, in
  this repository rather than a project of its own. It holds the blueprint,
  the machines, the cameras and the evidence log already, and it takes
  dependencies, so the coordinator is imported, never copied.

## Taken as the start

Defaults chosen here, each open to the owner:

- **vox rides in joe and otto in alfred**, as each is vendored today; neither
  serves a port of its own. A `vox serve`, or an otto of its own, is a member
  the moment it has routes.
- **Members on this machine only**, as carlos is now. A member on a machine at
  the printer is a revision this plan does not make, and the stays-local
  record's to allow.
- **The words.** *Blueprint* for the site as drawn and what it expects;
  *procedure* and *step* for the plan and its parts; *discipline* for what a
  step touches. None is in the page or the code today.
- **GO-only for matter**, and follow only for records, as *What fires* has it.

## The demo

Once the first three pull requests land, the simulated bench:

```bash
uv run apothecary serve --demo
uv run hearth check --hub http://127.0.0.1:6283
uv run hearth check http://127.0.0.1:6283/board --action '{"expect": {"node": "garage:esp32_blink"}}'
```

Then, in the viewer, Panels › Site › Procedure: a procedure on the garage of a
handful of steps, each a round trip through the hub as Loopback's are. A frame
kept from every camera. The devkit flashed with `esp32_blink` and its hello
expected, its port no longer unexpected in carlos's rack, held through the
hub. A print of a part sent to the simulated Ender, the step standing by at
`idle` and frames kept at every step of its progress. The footpedal's sketch
on the scripted Uno. The checks, `hearth check` on every member and `--hub`
on apothecary, as a step whose outcome is the verdicts. And joe's `say`,
answering not implemented until its verb lands, recorded as such and never as
a pass.
