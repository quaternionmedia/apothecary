## What
The record says its Consequences add to conformance "a cell↔angle mapping and
cases for it: placement order, the centre holding nothing, reachability of every
cell by direction alone, and the chord window on both sides." None of that is in
`conformance/vectors.json` yet (v0.3.0 has `geometry`, `time`, polar traces, the
ring ceiling, chord prefix-freeness and tempo). This edit adds a `cells` suite
generated from apothecary's implementation, so the two implementations cannot
drift apart quietly, and names the two changes `index.html` needs to pass it.

## Why now
Now, because apothecary has just built the second implementation, and the record's
last revision trigger — *a second implementation disagrees about placement order*
— is the one a conformance suite would catch before it becomes a disagreement
written into two codebases. It agrees today; the vectors make that a checked
fact rather than a coincidence.

## Seam
`tests/conformance/nine_cells.json` in apothecary is the source: `placement`
(`8 6 2 4 9 3 1 7`), `compass` (slot → cell, `8 9 6 3 2 1 4 7`, so
`angleToIndex(θ, 8)` names a cell without changing), `nearest` vectors for
arrow movement (occupied set, from-cell, direction → cell; at least twenty,
including the four-item ring where a walk along a row leaves a cardinal
unreachable and the nearest-in-direction rule does not), and `addresses`
(a path of labels through nested rings and the digit string that reaches it).
It is generated by the Python resolver and checked against it by a test, so a
change on either side shows up as a failing test rather than a stale file.

For rad, the same file drops in as `vectors.cells` with these cases in
`tests/conformance.spec.mjs`:

- **placement** — a ring of `k` items, `k` in 1…8, occupies exactly
  `placement[0:k]`; cell 5 is never occupied.
- **compass** — for each slot `s`, `angleToIndex(itemCenterDeg(s, 8), 8)` is
  `s` and the wedge's cell is `compass[s]`; a four-item ring drawn as eight
  compass wedges has its items at up, right, down, left.
- **nearest** — every vector, applied to a keyboard `move` event carrying a
  direction rather than ±1.
- **chord** — two directions inside `chordGapMs`-scale window land on the corner
  cell; the same two with a gap land there by walking.
- **hub** — a digit `5`, a press in `r₀`, or Backspace backs out one level and
  closes at the top, at every depth.

And the two changes to `index.html`: keyboard `move` events carry a direction
(`up/down/left/right`) or a `digit`, resolved by the same rule; the highlight
effect carries the cell alongside `i`, `id` and `label` — the record's clause 4
says a cell also identifies an item, and an effect that carries the index alone
makes the consumer re-derive it.

## Open questions
- Whether rad wants apothecary's JSON verbatim or a `cells` block inside its own
  `vectors.json` (the v0.3.0 layout suggests the latter; the content is the same).
- The chord window: the record says "a short window"; apothecary uses 150 ms.
  rad's `chordGapMs` is 30 for CharaChorder words, which is a different thing —
  machine-fast keys, not two arrows from one hand. One number should be named
  in the record.
- `dossier` is the first implementation and is not reachable from this session;
  its `nearest` behaviour should be run against the same vectors before either
  codebase is called conformant.
